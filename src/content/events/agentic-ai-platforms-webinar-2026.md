## Webinar: Agentic AI — Platforms, LLMs & Specialization

A webinar giving a practical map of today's agentic AI landscape: what an agent is made of, which platforms to build it with, how to choose between open and closed LLMs, and how to specialize a model for your own domain.

### 📊 [View the presentation slides](/events/agentic-ai-platforms-llms-specialization.html)

---

### Agenda

- What agentic AI is: components & types of agents
- Agent platforms: n8n, ElevenLabs, LangChain & many more
- Open-weighted vs. closed LLMs
- Running open models: Ollama, vLLM, llama.cpp
- Specializing an LLM: RAG, LoRA, QLoRA, full fine-tuning
- Case studies: real agents, real tuning recipes
