Chapter 01 · The definition

What is a CDN?

A network of servers in different geographical locations that deliver website content to users from a server closer to them.

🎯 A CDN helps to…
• Reduce loading time and latency
• Improve website speed and performance
• Handle high levels of traffic
• Reduce the workload on the origin server

Session wrap-up

Fast, reliable, secure — by being close

✅ Covered

What a CDN is, origin vs edge servers, and how request routing sends users to a nearby edge.

🛠️ Simulated

Cache hits and misses, push vs pull, broker failover, and what availability targets allow.

🌍 Applied

Security (DDoS, TLS, tokens), SaaS delivery, providers, and Netflix's Open Connect.

📜 Evolution
From static images and documents, to dynamic content and streaming, to edge computing inside the CDN.
⚠️ Challenges
Regions with weak infrastructure, hard-to-cache personalised content, and the cost of running many locations.
🔮 Future
Edge computing, AI predicting requests for smarter caching, and 5G enabling real-time delivery.
