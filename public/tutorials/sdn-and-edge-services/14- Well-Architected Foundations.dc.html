Chapter 01 · The idea

What the framework is

A clear set of best practices for designing, building and operating cloud systems — so your architecture is properly structured, optimised for performance, and aligned with what your application actually needs.

📦 What is a "workload"?

A specific application, system or set of components that delivers business value. It is typically the level at which business and technology leaders discuss design, performance and operational requirements — so it is the level the framework applies to.

Chapter 02 · A common misconception

It is not a checklist

✗ What it isn't
• A checklist of tasks to tick off
• An audit
• A compliance exercise
• A one-time gate you pass and forget
✓ What it is
• A structured, holistic approach to building systems
• Guidance for informed architectural decisions
• A collaborative, constructive discussion
• Continuously refined by real customer experience
💡 The point is not to pass — it is to understand the design decisions you have made, and improve them over time.
