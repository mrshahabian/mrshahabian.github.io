Chapter 05 · What peers tell each other

Route advertisement & filtering

UPDATE message AS 65002 → AS 65001
NLRI: 203.0.113.0/24, 198.51.100.0/24
AS_PATH: 65002 65010
NEXT_HOP: 192.0.2.1 MED: 20
📨 UPDATE contents
NLRI — the destination networks. Path attributes — the data used to choose the best route.
📦 Route aggregation
Combine prefixes into one summary — e.g. 10.1.0.0/24 … 10.1.3.0/24 advertised as 10.1.0.0/22. Smaller tables, better performance.
🚧 Prefix filtering
Inbound — block unwanted or incorrect routes coming in. Outbound — limit what you advertise to others.
⚠️ Why filtering matters: without inbound filters, one network mistakenly advertising someone else's prefixes can pull Internet traffic the wrong way — a "route leak". In SDN, filters are managed centrally from the controller.

Chapter 07 · Centralising the decisions

BGP in Software-Defined Networking

🧱 Traditional BGP
BGP runs independently on every router; each decides from its own local configuration. Changes are slow and hard to coordinate.
🧠 SDN-based BGP
A central controller with a full network view manages BGP decisions, talking to routers via southbound APIs and changing routes in real time.
Dynamic path selection
Monitors delay & congestion, updates attributes live.
Application-aware
Prioritises VoIP and video streaming.
Cross-layer
Combines BGP with MPLS for QoS and bandwidth.
Orchestration
Policy-based control of flows across many devices.
🏢 Multi-tenant clouds: each tenant gets isolated BGP sessions — often virtual BGP instances in software instead of physical routers — with per-tenant, policy-based routing and strict traffic segmentation.

Chapter 09 · From the Internet to the cloud

Cloud peering

A direct network connection between cloud providers, or between a provider and an enterprise — bypassing the public Internet.

⚡ Lower latency
No public Internet hops.
🔒 Higher security
Data stays on private networks.
💰 Cost efficiency
Fewer Internet transfer charges.
Four peering models
🔗 VPC peering — two VPCs privately, same or cross-region.
☁️ Inter-cloud peering — AWS ↔ Azure, for multi-cloud workloads.
🛤️ Direct (private) peering — dedicated physical link, no Internet at all.
🎯 Transit peering — a transit gateway joins many VPCs or clouds.
