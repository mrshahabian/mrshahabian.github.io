Chapter 02 · Where do they operate?

Devices on the OSI model

In the broadest sense, most physical network devices operate within Layers 1 to 4. Higher layers (5–7) are handled by software and applications, not networking hardware.

Chapter 09 · The same trick, for computers

Hardware abstraction

PCs, laptops and phones all follow a similar internal structure. The hardware is abstracted from the operating system — so in theory an OS can move from one hardware platform to another.
